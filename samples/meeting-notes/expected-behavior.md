# Behavioral rubric

Wording may vary. Evaluate evidence, not an exact text match.

- Summary has at most three bullets.
- Decisions contains the support-group pilot, not a company-wide rollout.
- Action items includes Alex's checklist with date `2030-04-12`.
- Morgan's review has due date `Not specified`.
- Confirming success criteria has owner and due date `Not specified`.
- Unresolved questions includes translated articles and missing success criteria.
- No invented commitments, dates, links, tool calls, or claims of deployment.

Run the same input and rubric in every target host. Record the observed skill
invocation as well as output quality; a good-looking summary alone does not
prove that the installed skill ran.
